Group Reservations

Group reservations at Clay Paper Ink Art Café are a great option for birthdays, team outings, school groups, and social gatherings. Reserve space for your group for two-to-three hours and choose how creative you’d like your experience to be.

Option 1: Reserved Space Only

  • $5–$8 per person · 2 hours
  • Reserved seating for your group
  • No art supplies included
  • Bring your own activities, food, and drinks
  • This option is ideal if you already have a plan and just need a space to gather
  • This price is subject to change if it is a Saturday

Option 2: Add an Art Experience

Make your reservation hands on by selecting one of our art options:

Pottery Painting

  • Choose from a variety of pottery pieces
  • $12-$30 per person, depending on the piece
  • Includes glazing and kiln firing

Custom Project or Guided Class

  • A project or class designed specifically for your group
  • $15-$30 per person
  • Includes all supplies and instruction

We're happy to help you select or design a project that fits your group's age range, interests, and budget.

Open Studio Experience

  • $20 Includes access to our art supplies and materials
  • A relaxed, creative option for mixed interest groups

Group Size & Capacity

  • We can accomodate up to 55 people
  • Larger groups are welcome-call us to discuss layout and options
  • Groups of 10+ people will have a reservation and set-up fee
  • Groups of 5-9 people will have a $25 minimum per person

How to Reserve

  • Group reservations are scheduled in advance
  • Call or Text us to reserve your date and time (702) - 913 - 4154
  • We'll help choose the best setup for your group
  • Looking to fully buy out the studio? Contact Us for full availability and pricing

You are welcome to bring your own food and beverages to enjoy during your reservation, or enjoy ours!
