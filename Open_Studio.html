Open Studio

Create at your own pace-no experience required!

Open Studio is a relaxed, drop-in art experience where you can explore a wide variety of materials and make whatever inspires you. Come solo, bring a friend, or make it a creative break in your day.

What's included

20$ plus tax for two hours of access to all of our shred art supplies

This includes supplies for Painting, Jewelry Making, Polymer Clay, Air Dry Clay, Drawing, and Sewing!

  • Good for beginners and experienced artists
